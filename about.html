OUR STORY

Learning with purpose

GACEB began in 2025, inspired by people with potential and the belief that learning can open doors.

Our purpose

GACEB Scholar brings Uganda curriculum learning resources, revision tools and study support together in one accessible place.

01 · Opportunity
Make useful study materials easier to find.

02 · Community
Support responsible learning and educational growth.

03 · Growth
Celebrate effort, progress and achievement.

Our commitment

We aim to make useful study materials easier to access, support teachers and learners, and encourage consistent study habits.
