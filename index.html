STUDY TIP OF THE DAY
QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Is GACEB Scholar free?

Core learning features are free. Optional paid services may be added later.

Is GACEB Scholar part of UNEB?

No. GACEB Scholar is an independent learning platform. It is not affiliated with UNEB and does not issue national examination results.

Where is my progress saved?

On this device, in your browser. It is not sent to any server. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Which levels and subjects are covered?

O-Level and A-Level. Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Biology practice is available now, and more subjects will follow.
