Thank you, from the bottom of our hearts

Behind every note shared, every quiz answered and every coin earned is a young person who refuses to give up on their future. Many of them study with little, and still they keep going.

When you give, whatever the amount, you tell that student something powerful: that someone they have never met believes in them. Your gift helps keep learning within reach, opens doors for learners who might otherwise be left behind, and honours the teachers who give so much of themselves.

We will never take your kindness for granted. We will use it carefully, honestly and with great respect for the trust you have placed in us. May what you give return to you many times over, in blessings, in joy and in the pride of knowing you helped build someone's tomorrow.

With deep gratitude,
Sr. Gauge and the whole GACEB family
